Field rules

  • Each row, column and rock stratum (color) holds exactly one fossil.
  • Fossils never touch, not even diagonally.
  • Mark rules out a cell. Stone sets a placeholder where you think a fossil might be, to test a line of reasoning. Dig excavates a fossil. Drag to mark or place stones on several cells.
  • Stones cost nothing. A red ring means a stone breaks a rule against another stone or a fossil.
  • Digging an empty cell costs one of three lives. Long-press any cell to dig while in Mark or Stone mode.
