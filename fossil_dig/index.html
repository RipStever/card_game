Field rules

  • Each row, column and rock stratum (color) holds exactly one fossil.
  • Fossils never touch, not even diagonally.
  • Mark rules out a cell. Drag to mark several. Dig excavates a fossil.
  • Digging an empty cell costs one of three lives. Long-press any cell to dig while in Mark mode.
